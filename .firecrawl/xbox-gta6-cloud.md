_Grand Theft Auto VI_, the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store, launches November 19. Considering the unprecedented demand, we encourage everyone to begin pre-loading as soon as possible to ensure you’re prepared for launch day.

_Grand Theft Auto VI_ will be playable on day one through XBOX Cloud Gaming on XBOX One and XBOX Series X\|S, starting at 1am ET. That means more players can experience one of the most anticipated games of the year on the consoles they already own. The game uses XBOX Series S graphics settings when cloud-streamed with XBOX Cloud Gaming.

Here’s how you can play:

- **XBOX Series X:** Play natively with the best XBOX console experience, [available now to pre-install](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10).
- **XBOX Series S:** The most affordable way to play _GTA VI_ natively on XBOX, [available now to pre-install](https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/k4ak9okk485712/grand-theft-auto-vi-pre-load-begins-on-november-10).
- **XBOX One:** Stream _Grand Theft Auto VI_ through XBOX Cloud Gaming on the XBOX One.

We’re excited to bring _Grand Theft Auto VI_ to more XBOX players at launch, and we will also be adding more titles from Rockstar Games to XBOX Cloud Gaming including _Grand Theft Auto V_, _Red Dead Redemption 2_, and more in the coming months.

Starting November 11, players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime to stream supported games without a Game Pass subscription. Streaming for _Grand Theft Auto VI_ begins at 1am ET on November 19.

Pre-order _Grand Theft Auto VI_ today on the [Microsoft Store](https://www.xbox.com/en-us/games/grand-theft-auto-vi).

_Cloud gaming availability, purchasing options, supported devices, account eligibility, and features may vary by market and device. Grand Theft Auto VI must be purchased separately._
