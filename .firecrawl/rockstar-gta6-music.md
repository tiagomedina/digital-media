### Coming December 11

The Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score is composed by Grammy Award winners Justice, the renowned French duo of Gaspard Augé and Xavier de Rosnay.

Justice bring their signature sound to this adaptive score, featuring expansive compositions all dynamically tuned to the different ways players engage with the world of Vice City and Leonida throughout Jason and Lucia’s story.

Coming December 11 to all major music streaming services as well as on CD and vinyl, the official Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score album features 30 tracks, along with a number of digital bonus tracks included across all formats.
