# Grand Theft Auto VI Now Available

November 19, 2026
Today we are proud to announce that [**Grand Theft Auto VI**](https://store.rockstargames.com/game/buy-gta-vi) is now available worldwide for PlayStation 5 and XBOX Series X\|S.

Vice City, USA. Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive.

Today’s the last day to get the **Vintage Vice City Pack** and a free month of GTA+ (which now contains [**GTAVI bonuses**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/gta-plus) for Members), included with [**purchase**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/editions).

Visit the [**official site**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) for official trailers, screenshots, artwork, and much more. There you can also [**download**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/media) the official printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover art poster and map of Leonida (look for a double-sided physical version of this poster to be made available at the Rockstar Store shortly after launch).

And be sure to check out IGN’s [**Official Grand Theft Auto VI Guide**](https://www.ign.com/wikis/gta-6), launching today with walkthroughs of the first two chapters, an interactive map of Leonida, and more.

Also out tonight at midnight ET, **Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album**, with 34 original tracks from a genre-defying roster of the most exciting artists in the world. Available on all major music streaming services and on vinyl or CD at [**gtavi-thealbum.com**](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/).

Please be mindful of openly sharing spoilers as we do want to ensure everyone can experience Jason and Lucia’s story for themselves.

Thank you again to our incredible community for all the support getting to this moment, we hope you enjoy Grand Theft Auto VI.
