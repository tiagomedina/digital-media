# Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album Now Available

### Listen Now on All Major Music Streaming Services, Also on Vinyl and CD at Select Retailers

November 20, 2026
[**Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album**](https://gtavi-thealbum.lnk.to/gtavithealbum), the official soundtrack album created in partnership with Atlantic Records, is available everywhere now. Stream it on all major music streaming services and get it on limited-edition vinyl and CD at [**gtavi-thealbum.com**](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/) and select retailers.

With 34 original songs, including brand-new tracks from Playboi Carti, Lana Del Rey, Don Toliver, Dua Lipa, JENNIE, JHAYCO, Zara Larsson, Gunna, Burna Boy, and more — Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album captures the electric energy of Vice City and Leonida.

To celebrate the launch, select artists from the soundtrack will also take the stage this weekend at [**Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/gtavi-thefestival) on Saturday, November 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami. The star-studded festival lineup includes Travis Scott, Cardi B, CA7RIEL & Paco Amoroso, Metro Boomin, Rauw Alejandro, Justice, Kodak Black, and Fcukers.

For the full tracklist and much more, including details on the Original Score composed by Justice coming December 11, visit the [**Music**](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/music) section of the official GTAVI site.
